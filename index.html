✿  A LETTER, JUST FOR YOU  ✿

Dearest Mum,

there's no card big enough for everything I'd like to say, so I made you a little garden instead.

thank you for the small and unseen things — the lunches packed, the worries you carried alone, the times you put yourself last. I noticed. I still notice.

I hope today you feel celebrated. I hope you eat something you didn't have to cook, and someone tells you you're beautiful (you are). most of all, I hope you feel — for one whole day — exactly the way you've always made me feel: completely, completely loved.

with all my love,

— ARIE
